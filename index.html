About

Built on weighbridges
and word.

A family business from MIDC Ambad — buying honestly, grading honestly, and moving metal for close to three decades.

Jai Bhawani Enterprises started in 1998 with one yard and a simple rule: the weighbridge doesn't lie, and neither do we. That rule hasn't changed.

Today we run two processing sites in MIDC Ambad with a crew of 25, our own trucks, and long-standing supply relationships with manufacturers across Maharashtra and Gujarat. Scrap comes in from factories and yard lots; graded, baled material goes out.

  • 1998
    Founded in Ambad, Nashik
    One yard, one weighbridge, one family.
  • Growth
    Second site & owned fleet
    Processing capacity doubled; three trucks on the road.
  • Today
    Two states, export groundwork
    Supplying MH & GJ manufacturers; building cross-border lines.

Process

One loop, run
for 28 years.

Buy, process, supply. Three steps, no middlemen theatrics — the same loop we've run since 1998.

Buy
